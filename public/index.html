How it works
1

Catalog

The addon scrapes the channel list from the source site and serves it as a Stremio TV catalog — with names and logos.

2

Stream extraction

When you pick a channel, the addon fetches that channel's page and extracts the embedded HLS stream URL in real time.

3

Playback

Stremio receives a fresh, time-limited .m3u8 URL and starts playback immediately — no buffering layers or intermediaries.
